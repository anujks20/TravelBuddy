3. The IoT Sensors We Are Making: Hardware & Remote Telemetry

When physical deployment begins in remote tourist locations, these custom-engineered IoT sensor pods provide autonomous, zero-grid intelligence back to the TravelBuddy platform.

🌊

Hydro-Sense Edge X1

Acoustic / Ultrasonic Hydrological Sensor

Continuously fires high-frequency acoustic soundwaves to measure waterfall plunge pool and stream depth elevation.

Target Location: Elephant Falls & Gorge Streams
Measurement: Stream Rise Velocity (+1.4m/hr)
Power Source: Solar + Supercapacitor (Zero Grid)
Telemetry Output: Water Level: Rising
👥

CrowdSense LiDAR Pod

Optical Edge AI Crowd Profiler

Privacy-preserving edge computer that counts footfall throughput with zero facial recognition or PII storage.

Target Location: Waterfall Entry Stairwells & Bridges
Measurement: 340 Persons/min Flow Rate
Edge Compute: ARM Cortex-M55 TinyML Inference
Telemetry Output: Current Crowd: HIGH
🌧️

Pluvio-Mesh Optical Gauge

Piezoelectric Kinetic Rain Gauge

Measures raindrop kinetic energy and instantaneous precipitation rates without moving parts that clog with mountain leaves.

Target Location: Ridge Topographic Zones (Shillong Peak)
Measurement: 48 mm/hr Rain Intensity
Storm Prediction: Cloudburst Pre-Alert Window: 15 min
Telemetry Output: Rain: Heavy
🛣️

GeoSlope Tilt & Moisture Probe

Subsurface Geotechnical Telemetry

Dual-axis inclinometer and soil dielectric permittivity sensor detecting hillside earth movements along transit roads.

Target Location: NH-6 Approach Roads & Hill Cut Routes
Measurement: Soil Saturation: 82% • Tilt: 0.14°
Warning Type: Mudslip / Slush Slippage Warning
Telemetry Output: Road Condition: Moderate
